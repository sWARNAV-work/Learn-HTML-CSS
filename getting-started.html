How to get started

Unlike many outdoor sports, getting started with bouldering is surprisingly simple. All you need is some climbing shoes, chalk, and a positive attitude--most gyms even rent the equipment for your first sessions. No prior experience or complex gear required!

The simple steps to getting started

  1. Find a bouldering gym near you.
  2. Rent or invest in a pair of climbing shoes.
  3. Learn basic bouldering techniques.
  4. Start with easy routes and gradually increase difficulty.
  5. Join a bouldering community or club.
